# TR Store Copy — English → Turkish pilot

AI-assisted storefront localization for one indie game or asset page: proposed **USD 39 for up to 500 English source words**. Bilingual CSV, clean Turkish text/JSON, small glossary and format-check report. One revision within the agreed source scope. Scope, deadline and payment method are agreed before work starts; no subscription or account access is required for a text handoff.

## Actual demonstration
This sample uses our own Queue Cues product copy, not paid client work. It contains 22 strings (249 approximate source words), including 3 explicitly synthetic formatting fixtures. No audio assets or outside game text are included.

| English | Turkish |
|---|---|
| Add to queue | Sıraya ekle |
| Remove from queue | Sıradan çıkar |
| Craft complete | Üretim tamamlandı |
| Upgrade unlocked | Yükseltmenin kilidi açıldı |
| {count} items remaining | {count} öğe kaldı |
| Progress: %d%% | İlerleme: %d%% |

Download the repository ZIP and open sample.html for the full bilingual view. delivery.tr.json and bilingual.csv are the actual example outputs. glossary.csv and REVIEW.txt explain contextual choices. format-checks.json records 127/127 mechanical checks on placeholders, markup, numeric facts, newlines and declared demo character budgets.

## Honest limits
AI performs translation and review. There is no independent human translator review or claim of native-human authorship, professional certification, customer credits, conversion gains, Steam approval or engine import. Mechanical checks are not language-quality certification. Store-copy translation does not mean the game supports Turkish. The 500-word scope is a pricing experiment, not validated demand or a revenue forecast. Do not send credentials or private customer/player data.

A pilot starts from text you are authorized to share, its page/context, preferred tone and any existing term list. Full-game narrative localization, live accounts, integration, medical/legal/certified translation and ongoing support are outside this pilot.
